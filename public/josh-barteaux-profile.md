## About This Platform

The CCO United platform was designed and built entirely by Josh Barteaux. It is a Next.js 14 application with a Salesforce backend, Monday.com event management integration, Playwright E2E test automation, GitHub Actions CI/CD, Resend email infrastructure, and dual AI assistants — Alisdelisgi (Anthropic Claude) and an Agentforce Einstein Bot. The Salesforce org runs on the Cherokee Nation orgfarm environment with custom objects, Apex triggers, LWC components, and a full BDD test suite.
